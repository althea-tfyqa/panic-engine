# Design Notes - Vintage Propaganda Aesthetic

## Reference Images Analysis

### Visual Style Elements:
1. **Color Palette**
   - Cream/beige paper background (#f5f1e8, #ebe4d1)
   - Black ink for text and illustrations
   - High contrast but WARM (not cyberpunk neon)
   - Aged paper feel, not stark white

2. **Typography**
   - Bold block lettering at card tops (outlined/inline style)
   - Hand-drawn quality - slightly imperfect
   - Typewriter fonts acceptable if black ink on cream
   - All caps for headers
   - Mix of bold display and body text

3. **Borders & Frames**
   - Thick black rounded borders (like flashcards)
   - 8-12px solid black with border-radius
   - Double borders in some cases
   - Card-based layout

4. **Textures & Details**
   - Cross-hatching in illustrations
   - Woodcut/engraving aesthetic
   - Organic, hand-drawn quality
   - Subtle paper grain/texture overlay (optional)

5. **Layout Approach**
   - Pamphlet/educational material aesthetic
   - Card-based sections
   - Think: 1920s-1950s moral panic literature
   - Church bulletins, school warnings, public health notices
   - NOT: Digital, slick, futuristic, cyberpunk

6. **Overall Vibe**
   - Vintage propaganda posters
   - Educational flashcards
   - Moral panic pamphlets distributed at churches/schools
   - Analog, printed, tactile
   - Warm, nostalgic but alarming
   - Satirical through authenticity

## What to AVOID:
- ❌ Neon green terminal text
- ❌ Black backgrounds
- ❌ Cyberpunk/Matrix aesthetic
- ❌ Digital/screen metaphors
- ❌ Glowing effects
- ❌ High-tech feel

## What to EMBRACE:
- ✅ Cream paper backgrounds
- ✅ Black ink aesthetic
- ✅ Hand-drawn/woodcut style
- ✅ Thick rounded borders
- ✅ Vintage pamphlet layout
- ✅ Warm, analog feel
- ✅ Educational card format
